What removing patient details does

On the day a list reaches its retention age, the patient name and phone number are cleared from every row and the prepared WhatsApp text is emptied. The MRN, doctor, department, appointment date and status stay, so campaign totals and per-doctor reports keep working. The patient directory and appointment history are separate records and are not affected.
